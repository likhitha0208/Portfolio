About Me

I'm a Network Engineer based in Bengaluru, India, currently troubleshooting and resolving Layer 2/Layer 3 incidents across TCP/IP, BGP, DHCP, and DNS in large-scale, multi-vendor LAN/WAN environments at AT&T. I combine hands-on infrastructure operations — root cause analysis, escalation management, and SLA/SLO-driven delivery — with Python and Linux scripting to automate network health checks, connectivity testing, and configuration compliance.

My background also includes an AI internship at L&T Technology Services, where I worked on OpenCV and deep learning for parking slot detection. I enjoy the intersection of networking and automation, and I'm always looking to build more resilient, self-monitoring infrastructure.
